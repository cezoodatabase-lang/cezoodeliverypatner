Privacy & Partner Rules

CEZOO Delivery Partner Policy

1. Protect Customer Privacy

Never share, save, photograph, copy or misuse a customer's phone number, address, order details or other personal information. Use customer information only to complete the assigned delivery.

2. Hand the Order to the Customer

Hand the order directly to the customer whenever possible. Do not leave it at a gate, roadside, security desk or another place unless the customer specifically asks you to do so.

3. Do Not Abandon an Accepted Order

After accepting an order, do not intentionally escape, abandon or ignore the delivery. If a genuine problem prevents delivery, contact CEZOO support.

4. Respect Every Customer

Speak politely and professionally. Address customers respectfully as Sir or Ma'am where appropriate. Abusive, threatening, discriminatory or inappropriate language is not allowed.

5. No Unnecessary Contact

Do not contact a customer after delivery unless it is necessary to resolve that delivery or CEZOO support instructs you to contact them.

6. Keep Customer Orders Safe

Keep groceries and other products clean, safe and undamaged during transportation. Never open, consume, replace or use customer products.

7. Verify Before Handover

Confirm the correct customer and order before handing it over. Do not intentionally hand an order to an unrelated person.

8. Collect Only the Correct Amount

For COD orders, collect only the amount shown in the app. Never demand extra money or personal payments from the customer.

9. Customer Location Is Private

Use the customer's address or location only to complete the assigned delivery. Do not save, share or reuse it for another purpose.

10. Maintain Professional Conduct

Harassment, fighting, unsafe riding, intoxicated delivery, smoking around customer products or other inappropriate conduct while on duty is prohibited.

11. Use Genuine Delivery Proof

Mark an order as delivered only after it has actually been handed over or completed according to the customer's instructions. Never submit false delivery proof.

12. Report Delivery Problems

If the customer is unreachable, the address is incorrect, an accident occurs or another issue prevents delivery, contact CEZOO support instead of abandoning the order.
